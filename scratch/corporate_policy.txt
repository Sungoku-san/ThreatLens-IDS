# CORPORATE SOC RESPONSE PLAN & PROTOCOLS

## Section 1: Overview
This corporate document establishes standard policies for detecting and isolating intrusion alerts on core switches, specifically for denial of service (DDoS) and brute force network incursions.

## Section 2: Denial of Service (DDoS) Ingress Rules
When a denial of service attack is detected:
1. Isolate the target network segment immediately.
2. Ingest the source IP using: `sudo iptables -A INPUT -s <attacker_ip> -j DROP`.
3. Configure SNORT alerts with: `drop tcp any any -> any any (flags:S; msg:"DDoS Alert"; sid:100002;)`.

## Section 3: Compliance & Audits Standards
Under ISO 27001 control A.12, all intrusion logs must be cataloged in a database, audited weekly, and outputted as signed incident PDF files.
